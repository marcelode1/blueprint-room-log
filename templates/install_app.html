🎙️ Narration Script (read while recording)

Intro: "Did you know you can turn ProjectONus into an app on your phone? No app store, no download — just four quick taps. Let me show you, first on iPhone, then on Android."

iPhone 1: "On your iPhone, open Safari and go to your ProjectONus link — the same one you use to log in."

iPhone 2: "Now tap the Share button — that's the square with the arrow at the bottom of the screen."

iPhone 3: "Scroll down a little and tap 'Add to Home Screen'."

iPhone 4: "You'll see the ProjectONus icon and name. Just tap 'Add' in the top corner."

iPhone 5: "And that's it — ProjectONus is now on your home screen, right next to your other apps."

Android 1: "On Android it's just as easy. Open Chrome and go to your ProjectONus link."

Android 2: "Tap the three dots menu in the top right corner."

Android 3: "Then tap 'Add to Home screen'. On some phones this says 'Install app' — same thing."

Android 4: "Confirm by tapping 'Add'."

Android 5: "Done! The ProjectONus icon is now on your home screen."

Outro: "From now on, one tap opens ProjectONus full screen — just like a real app. Same login, same projects, always up to date. Thanks for watching!"
